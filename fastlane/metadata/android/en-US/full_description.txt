LaWallet is a Lightning wallet built for communities. Get a Lightning address on your community's domain, pay and get paid in seconds, and tap a card to pay — all from one simple, private app.

Send and receive
• Pay Lightning addresses, invoices and LNURL in a couple of taps
• Receive at your own Lightning address or with an invoice for an exact amount
• See your balance in sats, BTC or your local currency

Real time
• Incoming and outgoing payments appear instantly, with an optional sound and notification

Cards and NFC
• Activate a LaWallet card by scanning its QR
• Charge an invoice by tapping a Bolt card on your phone
• Tap a voucher or card to claim sats (LNURL-withdraw)

Your keys, your wallet
• Sign in with a passkey, a Nostr key you already have, or a new key the app creates for you
• Payments go straight from your phone to your wallet over Nostr Wallet Connect (NWC); the community server never moves your money
• Connect any NWC wallet, or start with a free one
• Back up your secret key behind your device lock

Made for communities
• Works with any LaWallet instance — switch servers in Settings
• Open source (MIT): github.com/lawalletio/android-wallet
• No ads, no trackers
